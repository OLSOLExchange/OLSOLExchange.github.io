OLSOL Exchange FAQ: Tokenized Stocks, RWA, Security and Compliance

Frequently asked questions about OLSOL Exchange (Obsidian Ledger Solutions), tokenized stocks, Proof of Reserves, 24/7 trading and RWA products.

About OLSOL Exchange

What Is OLSOL Exchange (Obsidian Ledger Solutions)?

OLSOL Exchange (Obsidian Ledger Solutions) is a digital asset trading platform focused on crypto assets, RWA tokenized products and tokenized stock trading infrastructure.

Why Was OLSOL Exchange Created?

OLSOL Exchange was created to bridge traditional finance and digital assets, making global market access more flexible, programmable and transparent.

What Markets Does OLSOL Exchange Support?

OLSOL Exchange supports major crypto assets, RWA tokenized products, tokenized stocks and advanced derivatives-related services.

Tokenized Stocks

What Are Tokenized Stocks on OLSOL Exchange?

Tokenized stocks on OLSOL Exchange are blockchain-based digital representations of real underlying equities, such as U.S. or Hong Kong listed shares.

How Does OLSOL Exchange Back Tokenized Stocks 1:1?

OLSOL Exchange describes a 1:1 reserve model where each tokenized stock corresponds to real shares held through regulated custody arrangements.

Can Investors Receive Dividends Through OLSOL Exchange?

Tokenized stock holders may benefit from dividend-related distributions through automated mechanisms designed to preserve the economic characteristics of traditional equities.

What Is Fractional Ownership on OLSOL Exchange?

Fractional ownership on OLSOL Exchange allows users to access high-value assets in smaller units, lowering capital barriers for global investors.

Trading

Why Does OLSOL Exchange Support 24/7 Trading?

OLSOL Exchange supports 24/7 trading because blockchain-based markets are not limited by traditional exchange hours, weekends or holidays.

What Is T+0 Settlement on OLSOL Exchange?

T+0 settlement on OLSOL Exchange means ownership transfer and fund settlement can occur much faster than traditional securities settlement cycles.

How Does OLSOL Exchange Improve Capital Efficiency?

By using digital asset infrastructure, OLSOL Exchange aims to reduce delayed settlement, improve liquidity efficiency and support faster market response.

Security

How Does OLSOL Exchange Proof of Reserves Work?

OLSOL Exchange Proof of Reserves uses reserve verification concepts such as Merkle Tree structures to help users compare token circulation with reserve data.

What Security Measures Does OLSOL Exchange Use?

OLSOL Exchange highlights multi-signature cold storage, hot/cold wallet segregation, HSM encryption, 2FA and AI-based behavior monitoring.

Compliance

Is OLSOL Exchange (Obsidian Ledger Solutions) Regulated?

OLSOL Exchange states that it works with compliance-oriented entities and licensing frameworks, including MSB, NFA-related membership and VASP-related development.
