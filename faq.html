FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn the basics of tokenized stocks, RWA products, reserve transparency and OLSOL’s trading model.

What are tokenized stocks?

Tokenized stocks are blockchain-based digital representations of real underlying equities, such as U.S. or Hong Kong listed shares.

Are tokenized stocks backed 1:1?

OLSOL’s model is based on a 1:1 reserve system, where tokenized shares correspond to real shares held through custodial arrangements.

Can token holders receive dividends?

Token holders may benefit from dividend distributions through automated payout mechanisms, preserving the economic characteristics of traditional equities.

Why does 24/7 trading matter?

It allows users to respond to global developments outside traditional exchange hours, including nights, weekends and holidays.

How does Proof of Reserves work?

Merkle Tree reserve verification allows users to compare on-chain circulation with third-party audit or custody reports.

What future RWA products may be supported?

OLSOL’s roadmap includes tokenized commodities, on-chain Treasury products and DeFi collateral use cases.
