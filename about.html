About OLSOL

Obsidian Ledger Solutions

OLSOL Exchange is positioned as a compliant digital asset trading platform focused on RWA tokenization, professional trading infrastructure and user-first asset access.

Company Background

Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Denver, Colorado, OLSOL Exchange serves users across global markets with digital asset products, tokenized stocks and advanced trading services.

The platform emphasizes compliance, transparent reserves, hot/cold wallet segregation and institutional-grade operational standards.
